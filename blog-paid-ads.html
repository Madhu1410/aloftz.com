The uncomfortable irony of agency SEO

Here's something that doesn't get said enough. The businesses that are supposed to be best at digital marketing are often the worst at marketing themselves. Specifically, at getting found on Google.

We've seen it happen repeatedly. An agency runs excellent Google Ads and Meta Ads for clients, delivers strong results, and keeps those clients ranking. But their own website? Sitting on page three. No organic traffic. No inbound leads. Just a beautiful site that nobody finds.

When we audited 50 digital marketing agency websites, we weren't expecting to find much. We found everything. Every single site had at least four of the mistakes below. Several had all seven.

The 7 SEO mistakes killing agency rankings

Mistake 01
Targeting keywords no client would ever search
Most agency websites are optimised for phrases like "integrated omnichannel solutions" or "360-degree brand strategy." Real clients search for "best SEO agency in Kolkata" or "affordable social media marketing for small business." There's a gap between how agencies describe themselves and how buyers actually look for help. The agencies that close that gap own the first page.
Fix: build a keyword list from real client search queries, not internal jargon
Mistake 02
One generic service page for everything
A single "Services" page trying to rank for SEO, social media marketing, Google Ads, Meta Ads, web design, and creative — all at once — will rank for none of them. Google can't figure out what the page is actually about. Each service needs its own dedicated page with its own keyword focus, its own content, and its own intent signal. This is the single highest-impact structural fix most agencies can make.
Fix: create one dedicated landing page per service
Mistake 03
No local SEO — at all
Most agencies want clients from their city. But their website has no city name in the title tags, no Google Business Profile, no local schema markup, and no location-specific content. When someone in Mumbai or Kolkata types "digital marketing agency near me," the result they get won't be you. Local SEO is one of the fastest ways for a service business to start appearing in relevant, high-intent searches.
Fix: claim your Google Business Profile and add city-specific title tags
Mistake 04
Slow, heavy websites with no performance optimisation
An agency selling website development services, running a website that scores 38 on Google PageSpeed. This is more common than it should be. Core Web Vitals are a confirmed ranking factor. A slow website doesn't just frustrate visitors — it actively tells Google your site is lower quality than a competitor's. The fix isn't always a full rebuild. Often it's image compression, caching, and removing unnecessary scripts.
Fix: run a Core Web Vitals audit and address the top three issues first
Mistake 05
No blog — or a blog that hasn't been touched in two years
Content is still the backbone of organic SEO. Agencies that publish consistently — answering the questions their potential clients are actually searching — build topical authority that compounds over time. An abandoned blog sends the opposite signal. It tells Google the site is dormant, and it means you're missing hundreds of long-tail keyword opportunities that competitors are quietly capturing every month.
Fix: commit to 2 posts per month minimum, mapped to real keyword gaps
Mistake 06
Backlinks from nobody — or from the wrong places
Link building is still one of the strongest ranking signals Google uses. But most agency sites have either no backlinks or a handful from low-quality directories that do more harm than good. Earning genuine links — from industry publications, local business networks, guest articles, or client case study mentions — is how you build the domain authority that lets your pages compete for the keywords that actually bring in enquiries.
Fix: identify 5 credible sites in your niche and pitch one guest post to each
Mistake 07
Ignoring the technical foundation entirely
Broken internal links. Pages accidentally blocked from Google's crawlers. No XML sitemap submitted to Search Console. Duplicate title tags across half the site. These technical issues don't show up visually — the site looks fine — but they quietly suppress every other SEO effort you're making. Good content on a technically broken site is like putting a great product in a locked room. Nobody gets to it.
Fix: run a Screaming Frog crawl and fix crawl errors, duplicate tags, and redirect chains
None of these are new problems. Every one of them has a known fix. The agencies that rank well for their own keywords are simply the ones that bothered to apply them consistently.

FAQ: SEO for marketing agencies in 2026

Why do marketing agencies struggle with their own SEO?
The most common reason is that agency teams are fully occupied delivering results for clients. Their own website becomes an afterthought. The second reason is that agencies often write for other marketers rather than for their actual buyers — real business owners who search in plain language, not industry terms.
How long does it take to fix these SEO mistakes?
Technical and on-page fixes — title tags, page speed, site structure — can be done within two to four weeks. Content and backlink improvements take longer to show results, typically three to six months. But the compounding effect is real: agencies that fix these issues once rarely need to revisit them.
Should a digital marketing agency run Google Ads and Meta Ads alongside SEO?
Yes — and this is where the real leverage is. Google Ads and Meta Ads generate immediate visibility while SEO builds long-term organic presence. Running both means you're capturing high-intent traffic from search while also staying visible on social. At Aloftz, we integrate all three channels so they reinforce each other rather than compete for the same budget.
What's the first SEO fix a marketing agency should make?
Start with dedicated service pages. It's the structural change with the biggest impact on rankings, and it creates the foundation everything else builds on — content, backlinks, and local SEO all work better when your site architecture is clean and purposeful.
